# Open Math Problem Lists

A curated directory of open mathematics problem databases, subject-specific problem lists, formal conjecture repositories, and related research communities.

> Last verified: September 2, 2026  
> This directory collects public resources whose primary purpose is to list multiple research-level open problems. Inclusion does not endorse the current status of every problem. Always verify a problem against its original paper, the maintainer's notes, or a pinned repository revision before relying on it.

## Community and Discovery Sources

- [MathOverflow: open-problems](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/tagged/open-problems?sort=active): research-level questions, known results, and reference discussions.
- [MathOverflow: reference-request](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/tagged/reference-request?sort=active): requests for literature and subject-specific problem lists.
- [Operations Research Stack Exchange: open-problem](https://or.stackexchange.com/questions/tagged/open-problem): open problems and discussions in operations research.
- [arXiv search: “open problems”](https://arxiv.org/search/?query=%22open+problems%22&searchtype=all): recently published problem-list papers and workshop reports.
- [arXiv search: “problem list”](https://arxiv.org/search/?query=%22problem+list%22&searchtype=all): papers explicitly organized as problem lists.

## Verification Notes

- Problem lists maintained by research institutions, subject editors, or original proposers generally provide stronger evidence than aggregators.
- Aggregated databases, Wikipedia, MathOverflow, and search pages are useful discovery tools but cannot by themselves establish that a problem remains open.
- AI-oriented platforms, machine-generated status reports, personal sites, and old wikis require additional verification.
- A Lean proof only establishes the encoded formal statement. The formalization must still be checked against the original mathematical problem.
- When a source marks a problem as solved or disproved, verify the paper, review status, expert confirmation, and exact scope of the result.
